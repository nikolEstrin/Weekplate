# Weekplate Privacy Policy (draft)

_Last updated: [DATE]. Replace bracketed placeholders before publishing._

Weekplate ("the app") is a personal meal-planning app operated by [LEGAL NAME]
("we"). This policy explains what the app collects and why.

## Information we collect

- **Account information:** your email address and password. Passwords are handled
  by our authentication provider and stored only as a secure hash.
- **Your content:** the products, units, meals, meal plans, nutrition goals and
  shopping-list checks you create.
- **Technical data:** our hosting provider processes IP addresses and request
  information to operate and protect the service.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers. We do not
use analytics or advertising, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

## How we use it

Only to provide the app: to sign you in, store your data, and synchronize it
between your devices. We do not sell or share your data for advertising.

## Where it is stored

On your device, and in our cloud database hosted by Supabase ([REGION]), which acts
as our processor. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections.

## Retention and deletion

We keep your data until you delete your account. You can delete your account and
all associated cloud data at any time in the app: Settings → Account → Delete
account. You can export your data in the app: Settings → My data → Export.

## Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.
