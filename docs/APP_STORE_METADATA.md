## Description (EN)

Weekplate helps you plan what you eat — by day, week and month — and stay on top of your nutrition goals.

• Built-in product catalog with nutrition values, plus your own products and custom units
• Meals and recipes from ingredients, including nested meals and tags
• Daily and weekly planning, copy a day or week, smart meal swaps
• Calories, protein, carbs and fat against your goals, with "Balance day"
• Automatic shopping list from your plan
• Works offline — everything is saved on your device and syncs to your account
• Sync across devices, full data export, and in-app account deletion
